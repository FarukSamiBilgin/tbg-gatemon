# Extended abstract

**Status:** Manuscript in preparation (unpublished). Computational design study; no device has been fabricated. Coherence numbers are parametric in the quasiparticle fraction and 1/f noise amplitude, not absolute T1/T2 claims.


We present a design study for a gate-tunable transmon ("gatemon") built directly on the companion paper's verified platform: the v0.3 magic-angle twisted-bilayer-graphene (TBG) Josephson junction (I_c = 300 nA, E_J/h = 149.01 GHz) capacitively shunted into the transmon regime and controlled and read out through the same measured sub-terahertz silicon photonic-crystal cavity (f_cav = 145 GHz, in-situ Q_i = 1.461 × 10⁴).
A design scan over the charge-to-Josephson-energy ratio E_J/E_C locks at ratio 100 — the smallest ratio meeting the brief's own criterion (charge dispersion eps1 < 10 kHz and anharmonicity ≥ 0.5 GHz) — giving f01 = 40.597 GHz, anharmonicity −1.632 GHz and eps1 = 37.4 Hz, three and a half orders of magnitude (a factor 3156) below the ratio-50 hand-calculation point; at this design the superconducting-dome apex (ν = −2.4) is a double sweet spot, simultaneously the point of maximum E_J and of zero first-order gate-charge sensitivity.
Pinning the coupling operator to the Cooper-pair charge and to a Thévenin voltage-division model of a lumped collection electrode (H_int = 2βe·V_zpf·n̂, β = C_c/C_Σ, Koch *et al.*, PRA **76**, 042319 (2007)) strengthens the finding that the drive cavity cannot read the qubit out by 8.4×, and forces every readout budget to be recomputed with the shared charging-energy (C_Σ) budget of the two collection electrodes enforced. That recompute exposed a second, independent defect, adjudicated and corrected here: the dispersive shift χ had been evaluated everywhere with the detuning's sign inverted (Δ = f_r − f01 in place of the Koch Δ = f01 − f_r), a reference-inherited error that placed a spurious pole at f01 + |α| and overstated |χ| at the originally reported recommended point (f_r = 44.597 GHz) by 2.38×; corrected, that point's χ/κ is only 0.42–0.47, not the stored 1.01, it fails outright, and the entire f_r > f01 readout window is analytically empty (the Purcell/dispersive ceiling saturates at 2.943 MHz, below the cavity's own measured 3.052 MHz linewidth floor throughout the mandated detuning range). The two-cavity architecture survives only below the qubit's own frequency, inside the photonic bandgap that already protects f01: the corrected recommended point is f_r = 37.439 GHz, d_eff = 60 µm, χ/κ = 1.09 (Koch) / 1.00 (exact two-pole), at a readout-port efficiency of 30.7% — roughly three times the 11.5% the sign error had produced, which is withdrawn.
Every coherence number that follows is reported parametric in the residual quasiparticle fraction x_qp (and its effective temperature T_qp) or in the 1/f gate-charge-noise amplitude A, never as a locked value: **TBG coherence has never been measured.** The relaxation budget uses a full Catelani-type quasiparticle kernel evaluated at the design's own marginality ħω_q/Δ = 0.552 (a ×1.17249 correction on the relaxation *rate*, i.e. a T1 14.7% shorter than the small-frequency estimate, at every x_qp), a closed-form thermal floor, a frozen-parity dephasing bound 1536× longer than the qp-limited T1 at x_qp = 10⁻⁶ and T_qp = 20 mK, and a pair-breaking audit that confines the fixed 145 GHz photonic drive tone to an 11.7%-of-dome-halfwidth filling window — a *bulk*-gap margin only, per §9.1 below.
The dephasing budget replaces the milestone quasistatic estimate with Ramsey/echo filter-function integrals for 1/f noise S_ν(ω) = A²/|ω|, finding that the double-sweet-spot apex/off-apex contrast is 2041× quasistatically, 1292× under Ramsey and only 380× under echo — all specific to A = 10⁻⁴ and a 1 Hz acquisition bandwidth, and never quoted without that qualification.
The single-qubit gate-set analysis gives an honest coherent-error floor of 1.60 ns — not the leakage-only 1.01 ns a naive read would quote — and the same fixed-145-GHz-drive constraint that bounds pair breaking also confines gate-based multi-qubit frequency allocation to exactly one channel, with a per-qubit tracking drive as the only route to more (2–19 channels across both dependencies: 5–19 at a 10⁻³ leakage tolerance, 2–18 at 10⁻⁴, for gate times 1.011–8 ns).
We report the whole design as artifact-traced and parametric rather than as a set of locked performance numbers, and we flag three open items still unresolved — photon-assisted (multi-photon) pair breaking, higher-order-gap capture of the 145 GHz tone in the readout lattice, and the absence of any electromagnetic simulation at the readout lattice constant — plus one item a completed sensitivity check now quantifies rather than leaves open: replacing the transmon's sinusoidal potential with the measured non-sinusoidal Beenakker CPR shifts the anharmonicity by ≈ −56% and the charge dispersion eps1 by ≈ +182% at fixed E_J (τ = 0.8), while leaving the E_J/E_C = 100 design lock itself unchanged. A fabrication partner or a follow-on numerical study would need to close all four before this design can be built.
Five further stress tests (§9) probe the design against physics the production chain does not itself compute, and change the honest risk picture in both directions. Three surface new constraints: the junction's own Andreev sub-gap threshold — not the bulk gap the §5 pair-breaking audit checks — is already open over roughly a third of the E_J/E_C = 100 ground-state phase distribution at the dome apex (τ = 0.8, W = 0.33–0.37), so the fixed-145-GHz-drive scenario is one qubit with an open, rate-unbounded junction-level pair-breaking channel rather than a clean 1.37%-margin pass; an independent 1/f fluctuation of E_J itself (junction transparency, moiré-domain drift) dephases the apex with no symmetry protection at all — the missing third clause of the double-sweet-spot law — cutting the quoted apex T2* from 29.20 to 2.09 µs (Ramsey, with the filling-noise amplitude held fixed at A = 10⁻⁴) at a fractional E_J-noise amplitude of only A_EJ = 10⁻⁶; and the exported T1 stack omits shunt-pad dielectric loss and collection-electrode radiation, both of which outrank the quasiparticle channel at plausible parameters and cut the quasiparticle share of the total relaxation rate to 2.4%. Two tests instead strengthen the design as reported: the two qubits' shared readout cavity is itself a usable two-qubit coupler (J = 5.05 MHz, 5.7× inside the direct-capacitor dispersive budget of §8.4) whose missing piece is an OFF switch rather than a capacitor; and paper 1's own Shapiro drive point and this qubit are confirmed mutually exclusive on the one chip they share (n/n_crit = 1.8 at paper 1's operating point, needing 29 dB of drive extinction or a ≲10⁻³ duty cycle), while this paper's own gate tone leaves the qubit's coherence untouched.
